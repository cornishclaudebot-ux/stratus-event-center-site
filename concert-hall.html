Good to Know

What is the capacity of the Stratus Event Center concert hall?

Up to 2,250 guests for concert-style shows, one of the larger indoor rooms in Phoenix, with a built-in stage, dance floor and open, transformable main hall.

Where do I buy tickets for shows at Stratus?

Every show is listed with official sellers: Bandsintown, Ticketón, Tickeri and Ticketmaster, with verified resale on StubHub. The live calendar links each event straight to its official ticket page.

Can I book Stratus for a concert or touring show?

Yes. Promoters and artists book the room with in-house professional sound, programmable lighting and security already in place. Call or text or use the booking form and the events team will walk your date and production needs.

Does the venue have VIP seating?

Yes. Reserved VIP couch sections sit front and center of the stage: $200 for a table of four, $300 for a table of six, and $400 for the biggest sections seating eight to ten. Reserve a table.

Are concerts at Stratus all ages?

It varies by show. Each event listing states its age policy, for example All ages or Ages 18 & over, so check the event page before buying.
